Ordinary AI (unreleased)
    - The mod id is "specialai" again, as in Special AI. Mods and packs that look for Special AI by its id find
      this mod.
    - Coming from 0.0.1: rename config/ordinaryai.cfg to config/specialai.cfg to keep your settings. The reload
      command is now "/specialai reload".

Ordinary AI 0.0.1
    A port of Special AI 1.1.0 to the Cleanroom loader. Same features and options, under a new name.
    - Moving over from Special AI:
        - config/Special_AI.cfg is copied to config/ordinaryai.cfg the first time the mod runs. Every option keeps
          its name and meaning.
        - Mobs keep the AI they rolled under Special AI. Their saved data moves from the "sAI" tag to "ordinaryai".
        - Remove the Special AI jar; the two mods are not meant to run together.
    - New:
        - Options can be changed in game from the mod list, and reloaded on a server with "/ordinaryai reload".
          Changes apply straight away, except that what each mob rolled when it spawned is kept, and door
          breaking that is switched on later only reaches mobs as they are next loaded.
        - Clients no longer need the mod to join a server that has it.
        - Protection mods can veto mobs using blocks, as they already could for mobs breaking them.
    - Fixed:
        - Mobs only searched for things on one side of themselves (towards positive x, y and z). This affected
          explosion avoidance, calling for help on death, jockeys looking for mounts, animals looking for food,
          and the charge, shaman, spawner and throwing elites. All of these now look all around, which makes them
          noticeably more active than in Special AI 1.1.0 for 1.12.2.
        - Reputation particles could fail on dedicated servers. They are now ordinary particle packets.
        - Idle griefing destroyed farmland instead of trampling it to dirt.
        - Spawner elites spawned nothing if they were zombie pigmen or mobs from another mod.
        - Depacified animals re-rolled their temperament every time they were loaded.
        - Charge and barrage elites could go quiet for a long time after many attacks.
        - Mobs behind an archer no longer dodge arrows flying away from them.
        - Call-for-help no longer discards the allies a mob is set to ignore, and is not triggered by players in
          creative or spectator mode.
        - Jockeys no longer mount a mob they are fighting.
        - A mob pushed away from a block it was breaking stops instead of finishing the job from a distance, and
          the cracks on the block are cleared when the mob dies.
        - Config values outside their stated range are corrected instead of being used (a scan range of 0 crashed).
        - Mobs could be given their AI more than once when a chunk was reloaded quickly.
        - "/villageinfo" reports "No village nearby" instead of saying nothing.
    - Faster:
        - Removed a scan of every entity in every world on every tick (the thief elite's loot handling), a scan
          of every entity for every arrow fired, and a search around every mob every three ticks for explosives.
        - A mob that finds no way to flee an explosive, and a shaman that finds no ally, wait half a second to
          a second before trying again. The player-throwing elite judges crowds from one search instead of many.
        - Idle griefing and fiddling never load chunks, and one stand-in player per world is reused for block
          interactions instead of creating one each time.
        - Villages are frozen while unloaded through a small hook rather than by replacing the game's village
          storage, which also removes a source of conflicts with other mods.

Special AI history
1.1.0
    - Replaced vanilla door-breaking ai with one that is more configurable and works (slightly) better.
    - Added randomized villager names.
    - Trampling farmland now counts as breaking it for purposes of reputation/aggression.
    - Fixed the modifiers applied to elite ai equipment.
    - Fixed missing .lang file; /villageinfo command should work again. Also added anti-cheese config for the command.
    - Changes to configs:
        - Added scan delay option for idle interaction ais.
        - Added missing option for thief's cap speed modifier.
        - Discontinued support for block "metadata" in preparation for 1.13.
        - Now uses blockstate property notation, which offers better control and is more readable.
        - Default block target lists are now autogenerated, so they can potentially pick up mod blocks.
1.0.1
    - Fixed NPE crashes.
1.0.0
    - Somewhat rewritten. Version reset, either way.
    - The feature "special ai" renamed to "elite ai" to reduce confusion.
    - Added a few additional options for tweaking the balance of elite ais.
    - Shaman ai now triggers every 2 seconds from 1 second.
    - Shaman ai potion effects now all tier I:
        - Damage reduction reduced to 20% from 40%, speed reduced to 30% from 60%.
    - Shaman ai default potion effects now save to nbt to serve as an example for nbt-editing them.
